02 / UNDER THE SURFACE

A small vector.
A testable idea.

Real satellite embeddings. A deliberately simple model.
Every example, weight, and score is open.

01

Define a contrast

30 scenic landmarks and natural beauty examples, from Bryce Canyon to Crater Lake. 60 ordinary controls: 30 developed places and 30 open desert, grassland, or rural landscapes. Labels are subjective, selected by the author.

02

Find the direction

Read 2025 OlmoEarth v1.3 vectors at 10 m. Dequantize, remove local grid parity offsets, average each 160 m footprint and normalize. Subtract the ordinary mean from the scenic mean. Normalize once more.

w = unit(μ_scenic − μ_ordinary)
03

Follow it elsewhere

Score unlabelled footprints by their projection onto that direction. Exclude places within 5 km of any example and keep picks at least 1.5 km apart. Coordinates and place names never enter the scorer.

THE REALITY CHECK

Does it travel to a held-out region?

Six geographic groups. In each fold, fit the vector on five groups and test on the sixth. Separate held-out contrasts test scenic versus developed places and scenic versus ordinary open land. Candidate beauty still needs human evaluation.

—held-out AUROC
0.50 = chance · 1.00 = perfect separation
